## 2. Is the aggregation uniquely determined? No.

What the frozen record says:

- **D14** (`01B rulings.D14.estimands.normalized`) defines, **per arm**,
  `relative_log_gap = (R_log*(Z) − R_log*(X)) / (H(Y) − R_log*(X))` and
  `relative_brier_gap = (R_Brier*(Z) − R_Brier*(X)) / Var{eta(X)}`.
- **D16 E1a** makes the primary statistic *"block-clustered d=5 minus d=3 RELATIVE
  (signal-normalized) LOG-LOSS gap, i.e. the contrast in relative_log_gap as defined in
  D14.estimands.normalized"*.
- **D16 E1b** makes it *"within each scenario, average the B0 and B1 relative_log_gap
  contrasts; aggregate those scenario values to block means per D13"*.
- **D16 common_statistic.aggregation** points at **01A `contrast.aggregation`**:
  *"Replicates are averaged WITHIN scenario first; the scenario-pair difference is the
  observation. Replicates are never treated as independent units."*
- The advisor's own text (`ADVISOR_RULING_20260821`, D14) says only: *"Report both:
  1. raw absolute risk gaps; and 2. signal-normalized gaps"*, then gives the same two
  per-arm formulas.

01A's aggregation sentence is unambiguous **for a linear quantity**. `relative_log_gap` is a
**ratio**, and "average the replicates first" does not say whether the average is taken of
the ratio or inside it. Nothing in 01A, D13, D14 or D16 states:

1. at which level the division happens — per replicate (mean of ratios), per scenario after
   averaging numerator and denominator (ratio of means), or once per block after pooling;
2. whether the "normalized contrast" is the **difference of two per-arm ratios** (two
   different denominators) or the **raw contrast divided by one common denominator** (the
   d = 3 arm's, or the two arms' mean) — D14's formula is per-arm, but D16 never rules out
   the second reading in words, and it is the reading under which the headline flips sign;
3. whether the primary is pooled over the three `delta_eta` levels or reported per level
   (D14 mandates per-stratum reporting for `interaction_pairs` and is silent on `delta_eta`);
4. how the d = 3 arm's normalized value is to be obtained at all — the frozen twin stores a
   Monte-Carlo `representation_loss` and **no normalizer**, so D14's ratio cannot be read off
   any frozen artefact.

Three of the folding rules the acceptance check suspected turn out to be **immaterial to the
estimate**, and this is worth stating because it narrows the question:

| supposedly ambiguous | measured | conclusion |
|---|---|---|
| E1a's B0/B1/B2 collapse | `max|rel_log(B0) − rel_log(B1 or B2)| = 4.25e-14` over all 2,400 cells | the three widths induce one partition; the collapse cannot move anything |
| E1b's "average B0 and B1" — of the ratios, or of the numerators? | the denominator does not depend on the encoder, so mean-of-normalized **is** normalized-of-mean, exactly | immaterial |
| d = 3 arm recomputed exactly vs read from the frozen twin | A6 below: −0.01822 vs −0.01815 (E1a); −0.05185 vs −0.05182 (E1b) | immaterial at Monte-Carlo precision; also an independent check that the recomputation reproduces the frozen arm |
| `n_train` | absent from every population quantity | the 48 "scenario pairs" contain 24 literal duplicates |

What is left is exactly items 1–3 above.

## 4. Which choice matters, and how much

- **Ratio placement within the per-arm reading (A1/A2/A3/A6) is nearly inert**: E1a spans
  −0.0179 to −0.0196, E1b −0.0449 to −0.0595. Ordering the average and the division
  differently cannot explain a discrepancy of the size reported in §1.
- **Whether the contrast is normalized per arm or by one common denominator is decisive.**
  A4 and A5 are 10–20× larger in magnitude and **positive**: +0.348 and +0.194 for E1a.
  This is the single ambiguity that changes the scientific conclusion.
- **The `delta_eta` stratum matters at a factor of ~2** (E1a −0.0235 at Δ = 0 versus −0.0107
  at Δ = 0.3; E1b −0.0793 versus −0.0214), and the estimate is monotone in `delta_eta`. If
  the advisor wants a per-level primary, he must say which level.
- **Whether B2 enters E1b matters at a factor of ~1.8** (−0.0405 for B0 alone to −0.0725 for
  B2 alone). D16 already excludes B2 from the primary, so this is settled — but it shows the
  E1b headline is width-sensitive and the "secondary" widths are not decoration.
- **The inference unit moves `t` by a factor of 1.5–2.3** and can decide a q < 0.05 call
  without touching the estimate. That is D13's question, answered separately.

## 5. Does the qualitative finding survive?

The claim that matters is not the number; it is: **the normalized contrast has the opposite
sign to the raw contrast.**

- Under **every aggregation that computes D14's per-arm ratio and then differences the two
  arms** — A1, A2, A3, A6, all three `delta_eta` levels, every width folding, both
  `interaction_pairs` strata, and the normalized Brier companion — the normalized contrast
  is **negative** while the raw contrast is **positive** (E1a +0.00919, t = +4.64, 8/8
  blocks). **The sign reversal survives all of them.** For E1a that is 11 of 11 per-arm
  rows negative against a raw contrast positive in 8 of 8 blocks.
- Under the **common-denominator readings A4 and A5** the normalized contrast is
  **positive** and there is **no reversal at all**. The reversal is therefore *not*
  aggregation-proof in the wider sense: it is proof against every choice *inside* D14's
  per-arm formula, and it disappears under a reading that D14's formula excludes but D16's
  wording never explicitly forbids.
- One caveat on E1b: its raw contrast is +0.00129 with t = +0.62 (6/8 blocks). E1b is
  "raw ≈ 0, normalized negative" rather than a strong reversal. **The reversal claim rests
  on E1a**, where the raw contrast is unambiguous.

**Verdict.** The finding is robust in the sense that matters — it does not depend on how the
replicates, the `delta_eta` levels or the bucket widths are folded. It depends entirely on
one binary choice the advisor has not made: normalize each arm, or normalize the difference.
That choice is worth exactly one sentence from him and it flips the headline.

## 6. What is NOT reproduced

The acceptance check's independent figure — mean −0.0028, t = −0.58, 3 of 8 blocks positive
— is **not produced by any route in the table above**, and no combination of the enumerated
axes lands near it. Two structural coincidences, offered so the check can locate its own
route rather than as an explanation:

- `t = −0.58` is exactly the E1b `interaction_pairs = 0` per-stratum statistic (estimate
  −0.01247, 2 of **4** blocks positive) — a 4-block cut, not an 8-block one;
- `3 of 8 blocks positive` occurs at A1 restricted to `delta_eta = 0.3` (estimate −0.01069,
  t = −0.78) and at E1b A3 (−0.04493, t = −2.03).

Three further readings were tested and are also not it: hashing the d = 3 arm on its active
3-column space (+0.12228, t = +9.15, 8/8), using `H(Y)` alone as the denominator (+0.01324,
t = +4.64, 8/8), and normalizing the contrast by the d = 5 denominator (+0.12805, t = +4.88,
7/8) — all three positive, all three outside D14's formula. **The check's estimate agrees
with this document in sign and in significance verdict, and disagrees in magnitude by a
factor of ~6.** Until the check publishes its aggregation, its −0.0028 should not be quoted
beside the −0.0182 as though the two were competing measurements of the same estimand.
